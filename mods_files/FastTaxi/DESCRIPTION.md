---

## English

### Description
Shortens paid car/taxi extract wait times (typically ~60s → configurable).

### Settings
(`user/mods/FastTaxi/config.json`)
- **WaitTimeSeconds** (default: 8)

### Notes
Install: `user/mods/FastTaxi/`.
